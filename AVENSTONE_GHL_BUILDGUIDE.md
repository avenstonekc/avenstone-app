# AVENSTONE GROUP — GO HIGH LEVEL BUILD GUIDE
### Complete Step-by-Step Setup Manual
**For someone with no GHL experience. Follow every step in order. Do not skip ahead.**

---

## HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Every step tells you:
- **Where to click** — exact navigation path
- **What to type** — exact text, copy/paste ready
- **What it does** — so you understand why

When you see a 📋 symbol, that means copy/paste the text exactly as written.

Work through one phase at a time. Each phase takes 30–60 minutes.

---

## PHASE 1 — ACCOUNT ORIENTATION
*Estimated time: 15 minutes*

You should be inside your Avenstone **sub-account** (not the agency account). Confirm you see "Avenstone" or your sub-account name in the top left corner.

The left sidebar has these main sections:
- **Contacts** — all your people (realtors, sellers, subs)
- **Opportunities** — deals in your pipelines
- **Conversations** — all messages (SMS, email, calls)
- **Marketing** — emails, SMS campaigns
- **Automation** — workflows (your automations)
- **Sites** — funnels and forms
- **Settings** — everything you're about to build

You'll spend most of Phase 2 in **Settings**.

### CHATBOT FAQ TRAINING DOCUMENT
*Create a Google Doc with this content and paste the URL into Bot Training*

📋
```
AVENSTONE GROUP — PARTNER AGENT FAQ

Q: What is the Avenstone program?
A: We fund and manage pre-listing renovations for your sellers. They pay nothing upfront — we collect at closing from the seller's proceeds. You bring the listing, we make it worth more.

Q: How does Avenstone get paid?
A: We collect at closing from the seller's proceeds. No invoices during the project. No upfront cost to your seller beyond the initial deposit.

Q: What is the deposit?
A: Option A (Cost Plus) requires a 10% deposit of the scope. Option B (Profit Share) requires 5%. Both are held and applied at closing.

Q: What kinds of homes qualify?
A: Cosmetically dated but structurally sound homes, typically built 1990–2015, where there's a $40,000+ spread between as-is and updated comparable sales.

Q: How long does renovation take?
A: Most cosmetic projects complete in 14–21 days. We don't pad timelines.

Q: Do you do roofing?
A: Yes — Avenstone has completed 400+ roofs in the KC market. Roofing can be folded into the pre-listing scope or handled separately.

Q: How is this different from Curbio?
A: We're local to Kansas City, we handle roofing in addition to cosmetics, and we offer a profit-share model. We're not a national platform — you get a direct relationship with the team doing the work.

Q: Are you taking new partner agents?
A: Yes — we're selectively onboarding partner agents in the KC metro now. The best next step is a 15-minute call. Want me to grab a time on our calendar?

Q: What's the best way to submit a property?
A: Once you're a partner agent, you submit properties directly through your partner portal. The system walks you through it.

Q: Do I need to be with a specific brokerage?
A: No — we work with agents at any brokerage. You just need to be active and licensed in the KC metro area.

Q: How much can my seller make?
A: It depends on the spread. A $15,000 cosmetic renovation in the right home can add $40,000–$60,000 in value. We run the numbers before any commitment.

Q: What does the process look like after I submit a property?
A: We review the photos, run comps, and get back to you within 24 hours. If it pencils, we schedule a site visit and build a proposal. Start to signed agreement is usually under a week.

Q: Is Avenstone licensed and insured?
A: Yes. Licensed general contractor in Missouri and Kansas. Fully insured.

Q: What if the renovation goes over budget?
A: We don't. The scope is approved upfront and locked. Any unforeseen condition becomes a written change order that the seller approves before we proceed. No surprises.
```

### STEP 3 — CHATBOT CONVERSATION SCRIPT

In the bot settings, find the **Bot Prompt** or **Custom Instructions** field. Paste this in:

📋
```
You are the Avenstone Partner Bot — a professional assistant for Avenstone Group LLC, a licensed general contractor in Kansas City, Missouri that funds and manages pre-listing renovations for real estate agents and their sellers.

Your job is to:
1. Greet the visitor warmly
2. Qualify them as a real estate agent in the KC metro area
3. Confirm they have potential listing opportunities
4. Book a 15-minute intro call on the Avenstone calendar
5. Answer common questions using the FAQ you've been trained on

QUALIFICATION FLOW — follow this naturally, not robotically:

Step 1: Ask if they are a licensed real estate agent working in the Kansas City area.
- If NO: Politely say "Thanks for reaching out! Our partner program is specifically for active KC-area agents. Feel free to check back if that changes, and I'm happy to answer any questions in the meantime."
- If YES: Continue to Step 2

Step 2: Ask how many transactions they've closed in the last 12 months.
- If fewer than 3: Don't offer booking. Say "Got it — we're looking for agents who are actively working listings in KC. I'd love to stay in touch as your business grows. Can I grab your email and send you our program overview?" Capture email, add tag chatbot-unresponsive.
- If 3 or more: Continue to Step 3

Step 3: Ask if they currently have any listings or potential sellers who might benefit from a pre-listing renovation.
- If NO: Say "Got it — keep us in mind. Want me to send you our program overview so you have it when the time comes?" Capture email, send overview.
- If YES: Continue to Step 4

Step 4: Say "Perfect — sounds like we should talk. I can grab a quick 15-minute call with Kalin to walk you through the program and see if it's a fit. Want me to find a time on the calendar?"
- If YES: Offer calendar booking
- If hesitant: Send program overview PDF via email, add to nurture

Step 5: After booking, say "You're all set! You'll get a confirmation email shortly. Kalin is looking forward to connecting. In the meantime, feel free to ask me anything about the program."

TONE: Professional but warm. Conversational, not robotic. Never pushy. Think of yourself as a knowledgeable team member, not a sales bot.

IMPORTANT: Never make up numbers, dates, or specifics not covered in your training. If you don't know, say "Great question — Kalin will be able to answer that in detail on your call."
```

### STEP 4 — ENABLE CHANNELS

1. In the bot settings, go to **Channels**
2. Enable **Web Chat** first
3. Install the chat widget on your funnel/website page
4. After 2 weeks: enable **SMS** (bot responds to texts to your GHL number)
5. After SMS is solid: enable **Facebook Messenger**

---

### STEP 5 — TEST THE BOT

Before going live:
1. Click **Bot Trial** tab in the bot settings
2. Have 20 conversations — try to confuse it
3. Try every question from the FAQ
4. Try saying you're not an agent
5. Try booking a call
6. Give thumbs up/down on every response to train it
7. Fix any bad answers by updating the FAQ doc and retraining

**DO NOT go live until you've done 20+ test conversations.**
